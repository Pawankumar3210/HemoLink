HemoLink and Sustainable Development Goal 3

Good health and well-being for people of all ages.

The goal

The United Nations Sustainable Development Goals are a shared plan for a fairer, healthier world. Goal 3 focuses on healthy lives and well-being for all, which includes access to health services and support when people need it most.

Where HemoLink fits

Blood donation depends on voluntary donors, and families often start from scratch when they need one. HemoLink contributes by:

  • making voluntary donors easier to discover by blood group and place;
  • giving donors control over visibility, availability and contact;
  • reducing time lost to informal messaging chains; and
  • keeping coordination transparent, with moderation and an audit trail.

What this project claims, and what it does not

HemoLink is an academic coordination tool. It supports the spirit of SDG 3 at a small scale. It is not an official health service, it does not replace blood banks or emergency care, and it cannot confirm that a donor is medically fit. Always involve the treating hospital.

Future directions

Email and SMS notifications, hospital partnerships, donor verification and distance-based search could extend the impact while keeping the consent-first design.
