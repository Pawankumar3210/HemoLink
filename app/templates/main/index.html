How it works

Three steps, with consent at every stage.

  1. 1

    Register

    Create an account and complete your profile. Donors choose whether they appear in search.

  2. 2

    Connect

    Search for suitable donors or receive contact requests. Donors accept or decline each one.

  3. 3

    Make a difference

    Communicate and coordinate responsibly, then mark your request as fulfilled.

Why HemoLink

  • {{ icon('search', 24) }}

    Easy donor discovery

    Filter by blood group, name and availability, with results sorted so available donors come first.

  • {{ icon('pin', 24) }}

    Location-based search

    Narrow results by state and city. HemoLink never asks for a street address or GPS location.

  • {{ icon('lock', 24) }}

    Privacy-conscious contact

    Phone numbers stay hidden until a donor accepts a request, and donors can end access later.

  • {{ icon('toggle', 24) }}

    Donor availability management

    Donors set themselves as available, temporarily unavailable or unavailable, and can hide from search.

  • {{ icon('users', 24) }}

    Centralized coordination

    Requests, replies and status updates live in one place instead of scattered chats.

  • {{ icon('globe', 24) }}

    Accessible design

    Keyboard friendly, readable on phones, with clear labels and visible focus states.

Built for Sustainable Development Goal 3

SDG 3 is about good health and well-being for people of all ages. HemoLink supports that goal by making it easier for voluntary donors and people who need blood to find each other, using digital coordination and respect for consent.

Read how HemoLink aligns with SDG 3

HemoLink is not a healthcare institution, blood bank or emergency service, and it does not check anyone's medical eligibility.
