How HemoLink works

Two kinds of accounts, one consent-based flow.

If you want to donate

  1. Register as a donor with your blood group, age, state and city.
  2. Choose whether you appear in donor search. You can change this any time.
  3. Keep your availability up to date: available, temporarily unavailable or unavailable.
  4. When a recipient asks to contact you, read their message and accept or decline.
  5. If you accept, they can see your phone number. You can end that access later.
Become a donor

If you need a donor

  1. Register as a recipient.
  2. Post a blood request with the group, units, location and date needed.
  3. Search donors by blood group, state, city and availability.
  4. Send a short contact request. Requests are rate limited so donors are not flooded.
  5. When a donor accepts, their phone number appears on the request page. Then mark your request fulfilled.

Questions

Can I see a donor's phone number in search?

No. Search shows name, blood group, city, state and availability. The phone number is revealed only on your contact request page after the donor accepts.

Is a donor confirmed to be medically fit?

No. Availability is self-reported. A hospital or doctor decides whether a person can donate.

What if a donor declines?

Their details stay hidden. You can search for other donors. Repeated requests to a donor who declined are paused for a few days.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. From Account settings you can delete your account. Your personal details are removed. See the privacy policy for what is kept.
