Terms of use

Plain-language rules for using HemoLink.

Using HemoLink

  • You must provide accurate information and keep it up to date.
  • Donors must be at least 18 years old. Donating is voluntary and unpaid.
  • Do not use HemoLink to sell blood, to harass people, or to share another person's details.
  • Do not send contact requests unless you genuinely need a donor.

No medical service

HemoLink only helps people find each other. It does not verify identity or medical fitness, does not test or supply blood, and gives no medical advice. A hospital or doctor must decide whether a patient needs blood and whether a donor can safely donate.

Moderation

Administrators may reject blood requests or suspend accounts that break these terms or put others at risk.

Availability and liability

HemoLink is provided as an academic project, as is, with no guarantee of availability or that a donor will respond. To the extent allowed by law, the project team is not liable for outcomes of arrangements made through the platform.

Changes

These terms may change as the project evolves. Continued use means you accept the updated terms.
